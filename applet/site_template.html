What the models may look at

Eight RDKit descriptors, shared by the Trees and Neural networks tabs. Untick some and train again to see which matter.

Why the test set is stricter than usual

Test compounds are picked by molecular scaffold, so whole chemical series are held out. This mimics predicting the next series in a project, which is what Fang et al. (J. Chem. Inf. Model. 2023) argue matters. Data: the public Biogen ADME set, github.com/molecularinformatics/Computational-ADME.

MODEL FAMILY 3

A GPT writes one token at a time

GPT stands for generative pre-trained transformer. Its only job: look at everything so far and guess the next token. Here the text is SMILES, the line notation chemists use for molecules. ChatGPT is the same idea with far more text and a far bigger network.
